FAQ

The questions you'd ask anyway.

Are you an agency or one interpreter?

One dedicated interpreter — that's me. When you book me, I'm the one standing on-site. I can only be in one place at a time, so popular dates fill up fast and early booking is recommended. If I'm already booked, I'll personally refer professional interpreters I trust, with their direct contact details.

What does a day actually cost?

Half day $80–140, full sourcing day $99–250, trade-fair week $800–1,250. City travel and a written daily summary are always included. Exact figures depend on city, days and technical complexity.

What happens after I send my dates?

A written quote in USD within 12 hours — not a "let's hop on a call" reply. A 30% deposit locks the dates; the balance is due after the work.

What if you're already booked?

I'll tell you within the same 12-hour window and recommend a specific interpreter I personally trust, with their direct contact.

How far ahead should I book?

Two weeks is comfortable for most cities. For Canton Fair weeks and other major fairs, book 4–6 weeks out — those dates go first every season.

How do payments work?

30% deposit to confirm, balance after the work. Wire transfer, Wise, PayPal, or Alipay/WeChat for smaller engagements. All quotes in writing, in USD.
